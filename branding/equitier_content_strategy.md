# Equitier — Content Strategy & Video Ideas

**Core Niche:** Real Finance, Macro-economics, Wealth Architecture, Market Mechanics.
**Target Audience:** Smart professionals, early-career earners, and intelligent investors looking for long-term wealth, not get-rich-quick schemes.
**Tone:** Calm, honest, long-term focused. The antidote to loud "Finance Bros."

---

### The Boundary (What NOT to post)
* No startup equity or venture capital mechanics (that belongs on Cohort Zero).
* No crypto day-trading or technical analysis chart reading. 
* No "Side hustle" videos.

---

### 5 Video Concepts (Zero Overlap with other channels):

**1. The "Ghost Asset" Trap: Why Buying Land Isn't Always Wealth**
* **The Angle:** Speculative real estate vs. Productive real estate. Why millions of diaspora investors pour money into unverified housing plots that yield no rent, making it one of the riskiest assets disguised as the "safest."

**2. Sovereign Default: What Happens When a Country Goes Bankrupt?**
* **The Angle:** You are researching Sovereign Default (GDL paper). Use that! When a country defaults, what happens to the citizen's money in the bank? How does hyperinflation wipe out the middle class, and how do the wealthy protect their capital offshore?

**3. The Math of Boring: Why Day Traders Lose to "Lazy" Investors**
* **The Angle:** Address the trading illusion using game theory. Forex/Day-trading is a "Zero-Sum Game" (someone must lose for you to win). Investing in an index fund is a "Positive-Sum Game" (you capture human economic growth). Prove why 90% of active traders lose to passive indexers.

**4. The Illusion of the "High Income" Trap**
* **The Angle:** Break down the difference between Income (what you earn) and Capital (what you own). Why a doctor making $250k a year who buys liabilities (luxury cars) will eventually be overtaken by a $80k earner who buys yielding assets. 

**5. Inflation is a Stealth Tax on the Poor**
* **The Angle:** How governments use inflation to slowly erase their national debt by melting the purchasing power of the citizens' cash savings. Why holding cash is financially dangerous over a 10-year horizon.
