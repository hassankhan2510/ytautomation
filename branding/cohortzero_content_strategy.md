# Cohort Zero — Content Strategy & Video Ideas

**Core Niche:** Founders, Venture Capital, Business Strategy, Startup Case Studies.
**Target Audience:** Current founders, operators, tech employees wanting to build, and venture analysts.
**Tone:** Insider, analytical, strategic, and ruthless. Zero "guru hustle" motivation. Just pure mechanics of how companies win and lose.

---

### The Boundary (What NOT to post)
* No technical deep-dives on the code/physics of a product (that belongs on Syndar).
* No personal finance or public stock market investing advice (that belongs on Equitier).

---

### 5 Video Concepts (Zero Overlap with other channels):

**1. How Venture Capital Actually Works (The Founder's Secret)**
* **The Angle:** *You are 100% correct, this belongs here!* Demystify the tech world. Most people think startups make money by selling a product. Break down shares, dilution, how valuations are inflated, and how founders can be "worth" $10 Million on paper while taking a $70k salary.

**2. Why 90% of AI Startups Will Die in the Next 2 Years**
* **The Angle:** The concept of a "Moat." Explain why companies that are just "wrappers" around OpenAI will be crushed by ChatGPT updates, while deep-tech companies (who own their own hardware/sensors) will survive.

**3. The Anatomy of a Pivot: How Dying Startups Find Billions**
* **The Angle:** Look at famous startups that were failing until they completely changed their product (e.g., Slack was a failing video game company, Instagram was a messy check-in app). What is the exact framework founders use to know when to pivot vs when to quit?

**4. How Investors Spot a "Fake" Tech Founder**
* **The Angle:** Take the audience inside the mind of a VC. What are the red flags in a pitch deck? Why do investors hate founders who talk about TAM (Total Addressable Market) without having a Go-To-Market strategy? 

**5. The Theranos Boardroom: Where Ambition Crosses into Fraud**
* **The Angle:** Don't just say "Elizabeth Holmes lied." Break down the *business mechanics* of the fraud. How did she structure the board of directors to avoid oversight? Why didn't traditional healthcare VCs invest in her, while political figures did?
