# Syndar — Content Strategy & Video Ideas

**Core Niche:** Deep Tech, Physical AI, Machine Perception, Radar, and Autonomy. 
**Target Audience:** Engineers, deep-tech investors, defense contractors, mining operators, and robotics enthusiasts.
**Tone:** Authoritative, heavily researched, highly technical but accessible. No clickbait. This is a direct funnel to your startup.

---

### The Boundary (What NOT to post)
* No generic "Top 10 AI tools" videos.
* No software-only startup advice (that belongs on Cohort Zero).
* No consumer tech reviews (like iPhones or basic consumer drones).

---

### 5 Video Concepts (Zero Overlap with other channels):

**1. Why Autonomous Cars and Drones Go Blind (And How to Fix It)**
* **The Angle:** Cameras and LiDAR are amazing in perfect weather. But in a dusty mine, smoke, or heavy fog, they fail completely. Explain the physics of why optical sensors fail, and why *Radar-Native AI* is the only solution for true autonomy.

**2. The $10 Billion Space Race Nobody is Talking About**
* **The Angle:** Everyone talks about SpaceX rockets, but the real money is in SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) constellations like ICEYE. Explain how radar satellites map the Earth through clouds and darkness to monitor mining stockpiles and verify infrastructure.

**3. What Actually Happens When GPS is Jammed?**
* **The Angle:** A breakdown of "Denied Environments." In modern warfare or deep underground mines, GPS is dead. How do machines know where they are? Explain SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) and how robots navigate without satellites.

**4. The 50MB AI: Escaping the NVIDIA Trap**
* **The Angle:** Everyone thinks AI requires massive, power-hungry GPUs. Break down how "Physics-Informed Neural Networks" (PINNs) and optimized edge-compute allow advanced perception models to run on a tiny $5 chip without needing a massive NVIDIA rig.

**5. How to Digitally Weigh a Mountain from Space**
* **The Angle:** A deep dive into how Syndar's Phase 1 technology actually works. How do we use satellite interferometry to measure sub-centimeter shifts in mining tailings dams or estimate the volume of extracted copper?
