# TIL (Today I Learned) — Content Strategy & Video Ideas

**Core Niche:** Science, Physics, History, Human Body, and the World.
**Target Audience:** General curiosity seekers, TikTok/Shorts scrollers.
**Tone:** Fast, energetic, punchy. Hook in the first 2 seconds. State the crazy fact, then immediately explain the "WHY". 
**Format:** Strictly short-form (under 60 seconds).

---

### The Boundary (What NOT to post)
* No business, startup, or finance facts.
* No long-winded explanations. Keep it to one core mechanism per video.
* No generic trivia (e.g., "The capital of Australia is Canberra"). It MUST have a mind-blowing mechanism behind it.

---

### 5 Video Concepts (Zero Overlap with other channels):

**1. Why you can't hear anything in space (The Physics of Sound)**
* **The Hook:** "If the Sun exploded right now, it would be completely silent. Here is why."
* **The WHY:** Explain that sound requires physical atoms to bounce against each other (air/water). Space is a vacuum. Light and radar (electromagnetic waves) can travel through a vacuum, but sound waves cannot. 

**2. Why Mosquitoes Bite You but Ignore Your Friend**
* **The Hook:** "Are you a mosquito magnet while your friend never gets bitten? It's not about sweet blood."
* **The WHY:** Explain that mosquitoes track Carbon Dioxide plumes and specific skin bacteria signatures. They are literally smelling your metabolic exhaust.

**3. The Apollo 11 Computers were weaker than your Smartwatch**
* **The Hook:** "The computer that landed humans on the moon had less memory than a modern smart lightbulb."
* **The WHY:** Break down how the Apollo Guidance Computer used physical "Core Rope Memory" (wires woven by hand) to achieve perfect reliability, proving that brilliant math beats raw processing power.

**4. Why Glass is NOT a Slow-Moving Liquid**
* **The Hook:** "You've probably heard that old window glass is thicker at the bottom because it's slowly melting. That's a complete myth."
* **The WHY:** Explain the physics of an "amorphous solid." The real reason old windows are thicker at the bottom is just because medieval glassblowers couldn't make flat sheets, so they installed the heavy side at the bottom for stability.

**5. Why you get a "Brain Freeze" from Ice Cream**
* **The Hook:** "Your brain actually can't feel pain. So what exactly is a brain freeze?"
* **The WHY:** Explain that the cold hits the roof of your mouth, rapidly constricting blood vessels. A nerve misfires and sends a distress signal to your brain, making you feel the pain in your forehead instead of your mouth (referred pain).
