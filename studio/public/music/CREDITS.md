# Studio music library

Background beds for reels — documentary/history and tech. All tracks: **commercial use allowed**, **credit required** (CC BY 4.0).
When a reel uses a track, paste its credit line into the Instagram caption / YouTube description.

Rules: calm, cinematic, ambient, documentary beds only — no club / EDM / party music (see docs/MUSIC.md). Music is optional; voice-only is always fine.

## Edits
All beds were trimmed to 2:30 from the start, faded in/out and loudness-normalized to -20 LUFS for consistent levels under narration (Night Vigil: first 2s of silence removed). Originals: incompetech.com.

## Adding tracks
Only add tracks whose licence allows commercial use AND redistribution (CC0 / CC BY). Do NOT commit tracks whose terms forbid standalone redistribution (e.g. Pixabay, Mixkit, Scott Buckley) — this repo is public. Add an entry to `catalog.json` for every file.
