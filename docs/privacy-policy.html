Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nexaura Group ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our internal content-automation software (the "Application") that publishes content to social media pages we own and operate, including the LinkedIn Company Pages for Equitier, Cohort Zero, and Syndar.

1. Who this applies to

The Application is an internal tool used only by us to publish our own content to our own social media accounts. It is not a consumer-facing product, does not offer accounts to third parties, and does not process personal data belonging to members of the public.

2. Information we access

When authorised by us via LinkedIn's OAuth 2.0 sign-in, the Application accesses only what is required to publish posts to the LinkedIn Company Pages we administer. Through the LinkedIn Community Management API this may include:

  • An OAuth access/refresh token that authorises posting on our own behalf;
  • The unique identifiers (URNs) of the LinkedIn Company Pages we administer;
  • The content we choose to publish (video, images, documents, and captions we create).

The Application does not read, collect, store, or sell the personal data of LinkedIn members, our followers, or any other individuals.

3. How we use it

Access is used for a single purpose: to publish and schedule our own marketing and educational content to our own LinkedIn Company Pages. Tokens are used solely to authenticate these publishing requests.

4. Storage and security

Authentication tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in our private continuous-integration environment (GitHub Actions) and are never exposed in source code or logs. Access is limited to authorised operators. We retain tokens only for as long as the automation is active and revoke them when no longer needed.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share accessed data with third parties. Data is exchanged only with the relevant platform APIs (e.g. LinkedIn) as necessary to publish our content.

6. Third-party platforms

Publishing is performed through LinkedIn's official APIs and is subject to the LinkedIn Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review LinkedIn's policies for how they process data on their platform.

7. Your choices

Because the Application acts only on our own accounts, it does not create data subject to individual access or deletion requests from the public. If you believe content we have published affects you, please contact us using the details below.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
