# Channel Playbook — Syndar · Founders' Files · Compound

Your idea-generation bible for the 3 channels. Use it to pick topics that **rank, pay, and stay
credible** — never trash. Feed a chosen idea into the brief (see `AGENTS.md`) and the machine builds it.

> **The Trash Filter (applies to all 3).** Every video must do ONE of: *teach something real*,
> *tell a true story*, or *reveal real data*. If a title only sells fear, rage, or get-rich-quick
> hype and the video doesn't deliver substance — don't make it. High retention (people finishing)
> is what actually ranks you; substance is what earns premium CPM.

---

## The Idea Engine (never run out)

Any channel's ideas = **Pillar × Format × Trigger.**
- **Pillars** = the 3–5 recurring themes below (your evergreen backbone).
- **Formats** = *explainer* ("how X works"), *story* ("the rise/fall of X"), *why* ("why X happened"),
  *comparison* ("X vs Y"), *myth-bust* ("the truth about X"), *list-with-depth* ("3 X that Y").
- **Triggers** = what makes it timely: breaking news, an anniversary, a trending name, a question
  people keep asking, or a gap a competitor left shallow.

Mix any pillar with any format and a trigger → an endless, on-brand idea list.

## 1. Syndar — Deep Tech / Physical AI (`deeptech`)
**Promise:** high-signal breakdowns of how AI meets the physical world — and a funnel to Syndar.
**Audience:** engineers, founders, investors, defense/drone/mining/space pros. **Premium CPM.**
**Language:** global English. **⚠️ Human-verify every technical claim.**

**✅ DO:** first-principles engineering, physics of autonomy, sensing/perception, sim-to-real,
space & hardware. **❌ DON'T:** "Top 10 Scariest Robots", superficial tech news, doom-hype.

**Pillars:** (1) Perception & sensing (radar/lidar/camera), (2) Autonomy & robotics,
(3) Space & hardware engineering, (4) The Physical-AI thesis/industry, (5) Failure modes ("why X breaks").

- The physics of landing a rocket in zero visibility

- Why mining is going autonomous — and going blind
- The trillion-dollar shift: software AI → physical AI
- How self-driving hit the "perception wall"
- Why defense is betting on radar-native perception

**Funnel:** end every relevant video with the soft Syndar CTA + link in the description.

## 3. Compound — Real Finance (`finance`)
**Promise:** understand how money, markets, and the economy actually work — no hype, no stock tips.
**Audience:** investors, savers, the money-curious. **Premium CPM.** **Language:** global English.

**✅ DO:** how-money-works explainers, wealth psychology, economic history, markets demystified.
**❌ DON'T:** "make $10k/month online", stock picks, get-rich-quick, personalized advice
(**never** tell people what to buy — educate, don't advise; it's a legal + monetization line).

**Pillars:** (1) How money/markets work, (2) Wealth psychology & mistakes, (3) Economic history,
(4) Markets & instruments explained, (5) Big-picture economics (inflation, recessions, currency).

**Idea bank:**
- How inflation quietly steals your money

## 4. TIL (Today I Learned) — Curiosity / Facts (`facts`)
**Promise:** bite-sized "whoa, I didn't know that" facts. The mass-reach, near-autonomous channel.
**Audience:** everyone. **Language:** English + Urdu/Hindi. **Format:** shorts-first, daily. **Low CPM —
this is reach, subscribers, and top-of-funnel, not money.**

**✅ DO:** genuinely surprising, TRUE, shareable facts. **❌ DON'T:** fake/exaggerated "facts" (they get
community-noted and kill trust), rage/fear bait.

**Pillars:** (1) nature & animals, (2) space & universe, (3) the human body, (4) world/geography,
(5) history oddities.
**Format:** one punchy fact per short — hook → the fact → the "why it's wild".

**Idea bank:** "The animal that never dies", "Your body glows in the dark", "A planet made of diamond",
"Honey never spoils", "A day on Venus is longer than its year", "The Sahara was once green",
"Octopuses have three hearts", "There's a lake that turns animals to stone".

**Autonomy:** lowest stakes → the channel you can run almost fully hands-off (daily auto-short).
Keep facts *true* (a quick sanity check is enough — no deep verification like Syndar).

## How to turn an idea into a video

1. Pick a channel + an idea (or Pillar × Format × Trigger).
2. Brief the AI (from `AGENTS.md`):
   > Niche: `deeptech` · Topic: why cameras fail in the dark · Platform: youtube-long · Duration: 6 min · Angle: engineer-credible, funnel to Syndar
3. It researches → writes `script.json` (rank-fast title + kit) → validate → render.
4. **Syndar only:** you verify the technical claims before publishing.

## Cadence & reality
- Consistency beats volume — a steady 2–3 videos/week per channel outperforms sporadic bursts.
- Syndar = your money + funnel flagship (verify it). Founders' Files = your owned regional lane.
  Compound = your second money engine. Different audiences, all premium/credible.
