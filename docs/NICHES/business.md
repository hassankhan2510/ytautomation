# Niche Pack — Business / Startups

Apply this whenever `meta.niche` is `business`.

## Identity
- **channel:** `business`
- **accentColor:** `#e11d48` (sharp crimson) — for dramatic stories; use `#f59e0b` (amber) for lighter explainers
- **voice:** `en-US-GuyNeural` (authoritative) — or `en-US-ChristopherNeural` for tense stories
- **style string:** `dark-documentary`

## Tone & writing
- Sharp, analytical, a little dramatic. Business as human drama — ambition, greed, genius, collapse.
- Lead with stakes and money ("a nine billion dollar lie"). Numbers make it concrete.
- Cautionary and insightful, not preachy. End with the lesson the viewer can use.
- Factual and legally careful: allegations framed as allegations; stick to the public record.

## Visual bias (keywords)
Mix of image (specific: people, buildings, documents) and video (city/motion). Good seeds:
`city skyline night`, `modern office glass`, `stock market chart`, `money cash stacks`,
`boardroom meeting`, `factory production line`, `newspaper headline`, `courthouse`, `handshake deal`.

## Structure (long-form)
Hook (the shocking outcome or number) → who/what and the promise → the rise → the cracks →
the fall/turn → the aftermath → the lesson for founders/investors.

## Example topics
"How WeWork lost $40 billion", "The startup that faked its way to a billion", "Why Kodak died
holding the future", "The rise and fall of [company]", "The pricing trick that built [brand]".
