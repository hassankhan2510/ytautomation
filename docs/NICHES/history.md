# Niche Pack — History

Apply this whenever `meta.niche` is `history`.

## Identity
- **channel:** `history`
- **accentColor:** `#a16207` (bronze / sepia)
- **voice:** `en-GB-RyanNeural` (British documentary) — Urdu/Hindi: `ur-PK-AsadNeural` / `hi-IN-MadhurNeural`
- **style string:** `documentary`

## Tone & writing
- Cinematic, story-driven, a little dramatic. History as a gripping story with stakes.
- Open in the middle of tension ("In one night, an empire fell").
- Accuracy matters — history audiences catch errors. Research hard, cite sources.
- End with the lesson or the eerie parallel to today.

## Visual bias (keywords)
`ancient ruins`, `old map parchment`, `castle silhouette`, `vintage war photo`, `stone statue`,
`old manuscript`, `battlefield dawn`, `empire architecture`, `sepia crowd`. Prefer `image` (specific).

## Structure
Hook (a stunning moment) → the world before → the rise → the turning point → the fall → the legacy.

## Example topics
"The empire that vanished overnight", "How one letter started a world war", "The city buried in a day",
"The forgotten king who changed everything", "Why the Roman Empire really collapsed".
