# Niche Pack — AI & Tech

Apply this whenever `meta.niche` is `ai`.

## Identity
- **channel:** `ai`
- **accentColor:** `#8b5cf6` (electric violet)
- **voice:** `en-US-EricNeural` (crisp, modern) — for Urdu/Hindi use `ur-PK-AsadNeural` / `hi-IN-MadhurNeural`
- **style string:** `tech-explainer`

## Tone & writing
- Crisp, energetic, forward-looking. Explain the *so what*, not just the *what*.
- Cut hype; give the real capability and the real limitation.
- Make abstract tech concrete with one vivid example or number.
- Curiosity hooks: "This AI just did something nobody thought possible."

## Visual bias (keywords)
`neural network abstract`, `robot hand`, `data center servers`, `glowing circuit board`,
`ai chip closeup`, `code on screen`, `humanoid robot`, `hologram interface`, `futuristic city`.
Mix `video` (abstract motion) with `image` (specific products/people).

## Structure
Hook (a shocking capability) → what it is → how it works, simply → why it matters → what's next.

## Example topics
"What GPT-5 can actually do", "The AI that designs new drugs", "Why AI can't really reason yet",
"How self-driving cars see the world", "The race to build a thinking machine".
