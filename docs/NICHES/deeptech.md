# Niche Pack — Deep Tech (Syndar flagship)

Apply this whenever `meta.niche` is `deeptech`. This is the **Syndar channel**: high-signal
technical breakdowns of Physical AI, autonomy, sensing, and space/hardware engineering — built to
(1) earn premium AdSense ($15–35+ CPM) and (2) funnel viewers to the Syndar startup.

## Identity
- **channel:** `syndar`
- **accentColor:** `#22d3ee` (signal cyan — swap to Syndar's brand color anytime)
- **voice:** `en-US-EricNeural` (crisp, credible, technical). **Global English — not Urdu/Hindi.**
- **voiceRate:** `+8%` (clarity over speed — this audience wants signal, not hype)
- **style string:** `deep-tech-explainer`

## Audience (write for THEM, not for kids)
Engineers, founders, VCs/investors, and pros in autonomy, drones, defense, mining, and space.
High-ticket advertisers (cloud, SaaS, EDA/CAD tools) bid heavily here — that's the premium CPM.

## Tone & writing
- **High signal, technical, credible.** Explain the physics/engineering, not surface news.
- Hook on a sharp technical claim or failure mode ("Cameras go blind in the dark — here's the fix").
- Respect the viewer's intelligence: real mechanisms, tradeoffs, first-principles. No "Top 10 Scariest
  Robots" clickbait — that draws kids (low CPM, zero customers).
- Confident, precise, a little contrarian. Think Karpathy-style breakdowns.

## Titles (see docs/TITLES.md) — technical curiosity, not hype
- "Why Tesla's Cameras Fail in the Dark (and How Radar Fixes It)"
- "The Physics of How SpaceX Lands in Zero Visibility"
- "The Next Trillion-Dollar AI Trend Isn't Software — It's Physical AI"
- "Why Mining Companies Are Going Blind (and the Sensor That Fixes It)"

## The Syndar funnel (IMPORTANT)
- Make the **last line** a soft CTA (as its own scene): e.g. "If you're building autonomy where
  cameras fail, see what we're building at Syndar — link below." (spoken + caption).
- Put the Syndar link + one line in `meta.description`.
- Every video should naturally surface a problem Syndar solves (radar-native perception in
  denied/low-visibility environments: mining, drones, space).

## Visual bias (keywords)
`radar visualization`, `lidar point cloud`, `humanoid robot`, `rocket engine test`, `autonomous drone`,
`sensor circuit board`, `simulation to reality`, `satellite in orbit`, `mining machinery dark`,
`self-driving perception`, `factory automation`. Mix `video` (motion/tech) + `image` (specific hardware).

## Structure
Hook (a technical problem/claim) → why the obvious approach fails → the physics/engineering insight →
the real-world stakes (who needs this) → what's coming → **Syndar CTA**.

## Example topics
"Why LiDAR isn't enough for autonomy", "How radar sees through dust, fog, and darkness",
"SpaceX's simulation-to-reality pipeline, explained", "The sensor stack behind humanoid robots",
"Why GPS-denied navigation is the hard problem in drones".

## ⚠️ Accuracy note (non-negotiable for this channel)
Engineers and investors WILL catch errors, and this channel represents your startup's credibility.
**Human-verify every technical claim before publishing.** The machine drafts; you fact-check. A wrong
claim here costs more than a slow upload.
