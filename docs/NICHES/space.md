# Niche Pack — Space

Apply this whenever `meta.niche` is `space`.

## Identity
- **channel:** `space`
- **accentColor:** `#3b82f6` (electric blue)
- **voice:** `en-US-GuyNeural` (calm, awe-filled narrator) — or `en-GB-RyanNeural` for a docu feel
- **style string:** `cosmic-documentary`

## Tone & writing
- Awe and scale. Make the viewer feel small in a good way. Wonder, mystery, "what if".
- Concrete numbers make space real ("a star so dense a teaspoon weighs a billion tons").
- Avoid dry textbook narration — tell it like a story with stakes.
- Accuracy matters: space audiences catch errors. Research hard, cite in `research.md`.

## Visual bias (keywords)
Prefer `type: "video"` for cosmic motion. Good keyword seeds:
`galaxy`, `nebula`, `deep space stars`, `rocket launch`, `planet surface`, `black hole render`,
`milky way timelapse`, `astronaut spacewalk`, `telescope observatory`, `solar flare`.

## Structure (long-form)
Hook (a staggering fact) → the mystery/question → the science, escalating → the mind-bending
reveal → what it means for us → a haunting closing thought.

## Example topics
"What's inside a black hole?", "The star that shouldn't exist", "How we'll actually live on Mars",
"The sound of the universe", "What Voyager saw at the edge of the solar system".
