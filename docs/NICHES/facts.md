# Niche Pack — TIL (Today I Learned) / Curiosity

Apply this whenever `meta.niche` is `facts`. This is the **TIL** channel: mass-reach daily shorts —
but *valuable*, not throwaway trivia. The goal: stop the scroll AND leave the viewer smarter.

- **channel:** `til`
- **accentColor:** `#f59e0b` (amber)

- **style string:** `punchy-explainer`
- **platform:** `shorts` / `reel` (9:16), ~30–45s

## The differentiator (why this isn't generic trivia)
Most fact channels state a fact and stop → forgettable, "scroll past." **TIL always adds the WHY.**
Formula per short:
1. **Pattern-interrupt hook** — a claim that sounds wrong/impossible ("Your bones are stronger than steel — here's the catch").
2. **The fact** — stated crisply.
3. **The WHY** — the one-line mechanism/insight that makes it *click* (this is the value; it's what people screenshot and share).
4. **The snap** — a short "so that's why…" closer.

That "why" turns a fact into an *insight* — mass appeal + genuine value, so people follow instead of scroll.

## ✅ DO / ❌ DON'T
- ✅ counterintuitive, TRUE, "I never thought about it that way" facts with a real explanation.
- ❌ fake/exaggerated "facts" (get community-noted, kill trust), rage/fear bait, or bare trivia with no why.

## Pillars
(1) Your body/brain, (2) space & universe, (3) nature & animals, (4) everyday things you never
questioned, (5) history/world oddities — always with the mechanism.

## Hook styles (rotate — keeps the feed fresh, not same-y)
- "You've been doing X wrong your whole life…"
- "This shouldn't be possible, but…"
- "Nobody tells you why X actually happens…"
- "There's a reason X — and it's weirder than you think."

## Idea bank (fact + why)
- "Honey never spoils — because bacteria can't survive its water-starved, acidic chemistry."
- "You can't hum while holding your nose — because humming needs airflow out the nose."
- "A day on Venus is longer than its year — it spins that slowly."
- "Your stomach lining rebuilds every few days — or it would digest itself."
- "Bananas are radioactive — they're rich in potassium-40 (harmless, but real)."
- "Octopuses have three hearts — two pump to the gills, one to the body."

## Autonomy
Lowest stakes → fully automatable daily. Keep facts *true* (a quick sanity check), but no deep
verification like Syndar. The `why` is where the value lives — never skip it.
