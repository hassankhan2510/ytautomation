# Niche Pack — Science

Apply this whenever `meta.niche` is `science`.

## Identity
- **channel:** `science`
- **accentColor:** `#06b6d4` (cyan)
- **voice:** `en-US-GuyNeural` (clear, curious) — Urdu/Hindi: `ur-PK-AsadNeural` / `hi-IN-MadhurNeural`
- **style string:** `clean-explainer`

## Tone & writing
- Wonder + clarity. Make the invisible visible; make the complex feel simple.
- Hook on a counterintuitive fact ("Your body glows in the dark — you just can't see it").
- One idea per line; build the concept step by step. Accuracy is non-negotiable.
- End on the mind-bending implication.

## Visual bias (keywords)
`microscope cells`, `dna helix`, `laboratory glassware`, `chemical reaction`, `human body anatomy`,
`molecule 3d`, `physics experiment`, `bacteria closeup`, `brain scan`. Mix `image` + `video`.

## Structure
Hook (surprising fact) → the question → the science, escalating → the reveal → why it matters to you.

## Example topics
"Why time slows down near a black hole", "What actually happens when you sleep",
"The animal that can't die", "Why your brain deletes memories", "How vaccines really work".
