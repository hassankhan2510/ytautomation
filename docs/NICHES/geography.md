# Niche Pack — Geography & Countries

Apply this whenever `meta.niche` is `geography`.

## Identity
- **channel:** `geography`
- **accentColor:** `#14b8a6` (teal / earth)
- **voice:** `en-GB-RyanNeural` (documentary) — Urdu/Hindi: `ur-PK-AsadNeural` / `hi-IN-MadhurNeural`
- **style string:** `documentary`

## Tone & writing
- Curious, sweeping, fact-packed. Make places feel vivid and surprising.
- Hook on an astonishing fact ("There's a country where it's illegal to die").
- Great for the `map` block (place + coordinates) and `stat` block (population, size, records).
- Keep facts accurate; avoid stereotypes and politically loaded claims.

## Visual bias (keywords)
`aerial landscape drone`, `world map dark`, `mountain range`, `city skyline aerial`, `desert dunes`,
`ocean coastline`, `flag waving`, `crowded market street`, `satellite earth`. Mix `video` + `image`.

## Structure
Hook (a wild fact) → where/what → the surprising details → the human angle → the takeaway.

## Example topics
"The country that's disappearing", "Why this border makes no sense", "The most isolated place on Earth",
"5 countries you didn't know exist", "The city built on water".
