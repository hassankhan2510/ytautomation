# Niche Pack — Money & Finance

Apply this whenever `meta.niche` is `finance`.

## Identity
- **channel:** `finance`
- **accentColor:** `#10b981` (emerald / money green)
- **voice:** `en-US-GuyNeural` (authoritative) — Urdu/Hindi: `ur-PK-AsadNeural` / `hi-IN-MadhurNeural`
- **style string:** `clean-explainer`

## Tone & writing
- Clear, confident, useful. The viewer should leave a little smarter or richer.
- Lead with a number or a promise ("This one habit builds wealth quietly").
- Concrete > abstract: real figures, real examples, simple math.
- **Never give personalized investment advice** — explain concepts, frame as education.

## Visual bias (keywords)
`money cash stacks`, `stock market chart green`, `bank building`, `credit card`, `gold bars`,
`hand counting money`, `city financial district`, `piggy bank`, `rising graph`.

## Structure
Hook (a money truth) → the problem → the concept explained → a concrete example → the takeaway.

## Example topics
"Why saving money keeps you poor", "The 50/30/20 rule explained", "How compound interest really works",
"What the rich do differently with money", "The tax trick most people miss".
