# Niche Pack — Robotics & Physical AI

Apply this whenever `meta.niche` is `robotics`.

## Identity
- **channel:** `robotics`
- **accentColor:** `#f97316` (industrial orange)
- **voice:** `en-US-EricNeural` (crisp, modern) — Urdu/Hindi: `ur-PK-AsadNeural` / `hi-IN-MadhurNeural`
- **style string:** `tech-explainer`

## Tone & writing
- Futuristic but tangible — this is AI you can *touch*. A mix of awe and a little unease.
- Hook on something startling a robot just did ("This robot taught itself to walk in hours").
- Explain the *capability* and the *limit* honestly; skip the sci-fi hype.
- Make the mechanism concrete (sensors, actuators, learning) with one vivid example.
- End on where it's heading and what it means for people/jobs.

## Visual bias (keywords)
`humanoid robot`, `robotic arm factory`, `robot hand closeup`, `warehouse robots`, `robot dog walking`,
`drone swarm`, `mechanical joints gears`, `ai robot face`, `automated assembly line`, `exoskeleton`.
Prefer `video` for motion (robots moving) + `image` for specific machines/companies.

## Structure
Hook (a robot doing something wild) → what it is → how it actually works → why it matters →
what's coming next (and the human question).

## Example topics
"The robot that walks like a human", "Why every factory is going robotic", "Tesla's Optimus, explained",
"The rise of humanoid robots", "Will robots really take your job?", "How robot dogs learn to move".

## Note
Related to the `ai` niche (software AI) but distinct — `robotics` is **embodied / physical AI**
(hardware, movement, the real world). Use `ai` for models/software, `robotics` for machines.
