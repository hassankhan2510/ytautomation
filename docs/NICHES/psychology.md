# Niche Pack — Psychology & Self-Improvement

Apply this whenever `meta.niche` is `psychology`.

## Identity
- **channel:** `psychology`
- **accentColor:** `#ec4899` (magenta)
- **voice:** `en-US-AriaNeural` (warm, insightful) — Urdu/Hindi: `ur-PK-UzmaNeural` / `hi-IN-SwaraNeural`
- **style string:** `warm-explainer`

## Tone & writing
- Insightful, relatable, a little personal. The viewer should feel "this is about me."
- Hook on a truth about human behavior ("The reason you procrastinate isn't laziness").
- Use named effects/studies, but keep it human, not academic.
- End with something the viewer can actually do or notice about themselves.

## Visual bias (keywords)
`person thinking window`, `two faces silhouette`, `brain glowing`, `crowd blur`, `mirror reflection`,
`hands holding head`, `sunrise calm`, `people talking`, `alone in city`. Mostly `image`, emotive.

## Structure
Hook (a behavior truth) → why it happens → the psychology → a real example → what to do about it.

## Example topics
"Why you self-sabotage", "The psychology of first impressions", "How to actually stop overthinking",
"Why we crave what we can't have", "The dark side of being 'too nice'".
