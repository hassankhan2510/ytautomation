## Key facts (with sources)

- Theranos was founded by Elizabeth Holmes in 2003 after she dropped out of Stanford at 19;
  the company claimed its "Edison" device could run many tests on a single drop of blood.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theranos
- At its peak (~2014) Theranos was valued at roughly $9 billion; Holmes was celebrated as a
  young self-made billionaire. Source: https://www.sec.gov/news/press-release/2018-41
- The Wall Street Journal investigation by John Carreyrou (2015) exposed that the technology
  did not work and that most tests ran on modified third-party machines.
  Source: https://www.wsj.com/articles/theranos-has-struggled-with-blood-tests-1444881901
- In 2018 the SEC charged Holmes and Ramesh "Sunny" Balwani with massive fraud.
  Source: https://www.sec.gov/news/press-release/2018-41
- In 2022 Holmes was convicted on four counts of fraud; in late 2022 she was sentenced to
  more than 11 years in prison. Source: https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndca/pr/elizabeth-holmes-sentenced-more-11-years-defrauding-investors-theranos

## Angle / framing used

Cautionary tale: what happens when Silicon Valley "fake it till you make it" collides with
human biology — you can't patch a biological failure with a software update.
