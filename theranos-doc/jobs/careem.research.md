# Research — Careem (Pakistan startup story)

- Careem was co-founded in 2012; co-founder Mudassir Sheikha is Pakistani. It became a leading
  ride-hailing service across the Middle East and Pakistan.
  Source: https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/technology-media-and-telecommunications/our-insights/whats-fueling-pakistans-emerging-start-up-ecosystem
- Uber acquired Careem for over $3 billion (announced 2019, closed early 2020).
  Source: https://endeavor.org/stories/pakistan-indonesia/
- Former Careem staff went on to start 100+ new businesses across the region — the
  "Careem Mafia" — a major source of entrepreneurial spillover.
  Source: https://www.digitaleye.pk/pakistan/where-is-the-careems-start-up-generation-today-following-its-3-1-billion-sale/
- Careem suspended its Pakistan ride-hailing service in July 2025 amid fuel-price volatility,
  inflation and competition (inDrive, Yango).
  Source: https://insights.datadarbar.io/pakistan-startup-funding-2025-teaser/

Angle: the $3.1B exit that sparked a generation of Pakistani founders.
