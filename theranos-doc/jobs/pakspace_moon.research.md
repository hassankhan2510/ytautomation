# Research — Pakistan's Moon Mission (iCube-Qamar)

- iCube-Qamar is Pakistan's first lunar satellite, launched 3 May 2024 from Hainan, China, aboard
  China's Chang'e-6 mission. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICUBE-Q
- It entered lunar orbit on 8 May 2024. Source: https://www.dawn.com/news/1832658
- Designed and developed by Pakistan's Institute of Space Technology (IST) with Shanghai Jiao Tong
  University (SJTU) and national space agency SUPARCO.
  Source: https://thefridaytimes.com/03-May-2024/icube-qamar-pakistan-launches-first-lunar-mission
- It carries two cameras and imaged the Moon from ~200 km, giving Pakistan its own lunar imagery.
  Source: https://arynews.tv/icube-qamar-pakistans-satellite-sends-first-image

Angle: a small-budget cubesat put Pakistan on the Moon for the first time.
