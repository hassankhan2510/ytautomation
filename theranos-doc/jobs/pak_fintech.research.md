# Research — Pakistan's Fintech Boom

- Fintech dominates Pakistani startup funding: in 2024, fintech secured $30.5M — over 80% of all
  capital deployed that year.
  Source: https://insights.datadarbar.io/pakistan-startup-funding-2025-teaser/
- Since 2015, ~450 fintech companies have raised about $391M in venture capital in Pakistan.
  Source: https://insights.datadarbar.io/pakistan-startup-funding-2025-teaser/
- SadaPay raised a $7.2M seed round — reported as the largest-ever seed by a Pakistani startup —
  and was later acquired by Turkey's Papara.
  Source: https://kickstart.pk/12-top-start-ups-that-raised-investment/
- The ecosystem is openly speculating about who becomes Pakistan's first unicorn.
  Source: https://startup.pk/pakistan-startup-ecosystem-who-will-become-pakistans-first-unicorn/

Angle: money is going digital in Pakistan, and fintech is leading the race to the first unicorn.
