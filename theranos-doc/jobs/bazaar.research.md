# Research — Bazaar (Pakistan startup story)

- Bazaar was founded in 2020 by childhood friends Hamza Jawaid and Saad Jangda; it is a B2B
  marketplace serving small kiryana (grocery) stores.
  Source: https://kickstart.pk/12-top-start-ups-that-raised-investment/
- Bazaar raised a $30M Series A in 2021, one of Pakistan's largest rounds.
  Source: https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/technology-media-and-telecommunications/our-insights/whats-fueling-pakistans-emerging-start-up-ecosystem
- In March 2025 Bazaar acquired fintech company Keenu, extending into payments — cited as
  consolidation-led growth in Pakistan's fintech layer.
  Source: https://insights.datadarbar.io/pakistan-startup-funding-2025-teaser/
- Bazaar is ranked among the top Pakistani startups.
  Source: https://tracxn.com/d/geographies/pakistan

Angle: two friends digitizing Pakistan's corner shops into a tech + fintech platform.
