# Research — Pakistan's Space Journey (SUPARCO)

- Pakistan's space program began in 1961; SUPARCO was formally established in 1964. Its first
  head was Nobel laureate Dr. Abdus Salam. Pakistan was among the first ~10 nations to start a
  space program. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SUPARCO
- In 1962, SUPARCO launched its first rocket, Rehbar-I, from near Karachi — third in Asia to do so.
  Source: https://newspaceeconomy.ca/2025/03/01/pakistans-space-journey-the-story-of-suparco/
- PAKSAT-MM1, Pakistan's first multi-mission (communications) satellite, launched 30 May 2024 and
  is designed to operate 15+ years. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paksat-MM1
- iCube-Qamar reached lunar orbit in May 2024 (Pakistan's first Moon mission).
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICUBE-Q
- In Feb 2025 Pakistan and China agreed to send Pakistan's first astronaut to the Tiangong space
  station — the first foreign national aboard it — tentatively around October 2026.
  Source: https://spacenews.com/china-to-train-pakistani-astronaut-for-tiangong-space-station-mission/

Angle: from a 1961 rocket program to a planned 2026 astronaut — Pakistan's quiet climb to the stars.
